Overview

Built for agri-sales on the ground

Samparkasetu is built to empower OEMs, dealers, and distributors with the most practical digital sales platform in agri-tech — simple enough for a field agent, yet powerful enough for an administrator.

The platform combines a tailored CRM, intelligent territory mapping, and AI-powered lead qualification, all built specifically for the realities of agri-sales on the ground.

Our pilot is live in Karnataka, working with dealers and distributors representing major tractor OEM brands across the state. As of June 2026, we have achieved a 100% trial-to-subscription conversion rate and 100% dealer retention.

Our vision is to empower every OEM, distributor, and dealer across agri-India to transition from conventional field sales to structured, data-driven digital sales.
