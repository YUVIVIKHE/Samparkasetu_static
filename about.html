The Story

Why we built Samparkasetu

India's agriculture sector employs over 600 million people and contributes nearly 20% of GDP. Yet, the technology available to the businesses and people within it was fragmented, expensive, or simply absent.

Dealers were managing leads on paper. Manufacturers had no visibility into district-level demand. Farmers had no way to compare products, prices, or financing options. The entire ecosystem was operating well below its potential.

Samparkasetu was founded to change that. We built a unified AI-powered platform that connects every participant in the agriculture value chain — from the seed company to the farmer's field, from the tractor OEM to the last-mile dealer, from the bank to the borrower.

The name Samparkasetu means "bridge of connection" in Sanskrit — and that is exactly what we are. The bridge that brings India's agriculture into the digital age.
